Five hero treatments.

Each one sits in a mock of the real hero, at the real proportions, with the real screens. Resize the window and try them on a phone. Hover the deck.

1

Cross-fade carousel

One frame, 4 screens, a slow cross-fade every 3.5 seconds. Safe and cheap. Nothing moves except the pixels inside the phone, so the headline never shifts. The cost is that it shows one screen at a time, so anyone who looks for 2 seconds sees exactly one thing.

2

Fanned deck

Four phones in depth, the front one sharp and the rest dimmed behind. Hover it. The whole thing reads with no motion at all, which is the point: most people see a hero for 2 seconds, and this one is complete in a still frame. The back screens are texture rather than information.

3

The film, inside the phone

The launch film playing in the frame, muted and looping. This is the cheap version, and you can see the problem: the film is 16:9 and the phone is 9:19.5, so the crop throws away most of every frame. Doing this properly means re-cutting the film vertically, which is a second render of the whole composition.

4

Scroll-linked swap

The screen changes as the reader scrolls rather than on a timer. Drag the slider to feel it, since a demo page cannot give this one its real scroll distance. Motion tied to intent feels deliberate, but it is invisible to anyone who never scrolls, which is exactly who a hero is for.

5

Live working timer

Not a screenshot. A real timer running 20/10 by 8, the ring draining, the colour flipping magenta to teal. It never repeats and it proves the idea in 3 seconds. The risk is upkeep: the day the app's timer screen changes, this hero silently becomes a picture of a product that no longer exists.

2 is the one I would ship. It is the only treatment that is finished the instant it appears, and a hero gets about 2 seconds. 1 is the safe default and nobody will ever dislike it. 5 is the most exciting and the most fragile. 3 needs a vertical recut before it is fair to judge. 4 is elegant but spends its trick on people who already scrolled.
