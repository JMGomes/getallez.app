Three directions for the Allez site.

Each one is a complete, working page built from the real app screens and the launch film. They share the brand palette, the typeface and the existing page structure, so whichever you pick, the privacy and support pages still fit. Open them on a phone too.

Interval

The page behaves like the app. The whole background changes colour as you scroll through the sections, a live Tabata runs in the hero, and the film sits on black in the middle.

Console

The app as a precision instrument. Near black, tight grid, mono labels, a real chart of a training week, and the film autoplaying muted as the hero with sound on request.

Bento

Light and playful. A tile grid where the film takes the biggest cell, tiles tilt toward the pointer, and one corner tile runs a live Tabata. Follows the system light or dark setting.
