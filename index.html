A look inside

Built for the middle of a burpee

Made to be heard, not watched.

Cues that keep playing

A sound at every change, a 3-2-1 countdown before it, and a chime at the end. They keep going with the screen locked and the app in the background.

Beeps or a voice

Four beep styles: Classic, Wood, Digital and Chime. Or spoken cues, "Work", "Rest", "Last one", in a voice from your phone.

On your Lock Screen

On iPhone the interval and its countdown live in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. On Android a notification shows them, with Pause and Resume.

Under your music

Your playlist dips for each cue and comes straight back. Nothing stops, nothing needs a separate volume.

Yours to keep

Routines, history and settings live on your device and nowhere else. No account, no analytics, no ads. It works on a plane.

Sweat-proof controls

Big targets you can hit without looking. Lock them so a knee never pauses your set.
